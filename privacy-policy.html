Architect Assistant — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Architect Assistant is a browser extension that runs entirely in your browser. The developer operates no servers and receives no data from you.

Salesforce session

The extension reads your existing Salesforce session cookie for the org you choose to connect to, and uses it only to call that org's APIs from your browser. It is held in memory only and is never sent to the developer or any third party.

Salesforce data

Metadata and query results are fetched directly from your org to your browser and shown to you. They are not transmitted to the developer.

Optional AI features

If you enter an API key for Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq or Google Gemini, then when you ask a question the extension sends your question and the org context needed to answer it (metadata and, only if you enable it, record values) directly from your browser to the provider you selected. That provider's privacy policy applies. Your key is kept in session storage and is cleared when you close the browser. Nothing is sent unless you ask.

Local storage

Preferences, favorites, tags and history are stored on your device only and are removed when you uninstall the extension.

No sale or sharing

We do not sell or share your data, and do not use it for advertising, creditworthiness or any purpose other than the features described above.
