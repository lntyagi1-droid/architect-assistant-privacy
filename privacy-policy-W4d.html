Workbench4Dev — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 Applies to: the Workbench4Dev Chrome extension (all versions)

Workbench4Dev is a browser extension that helps Salesforce admins, developers and architects explore and analyze their own Salesforce org (metadata, schema, permissions, Apex, debug logs and more) using the Salesforce session already open in their browser.

The short version: Workbench4Dev works entirely inside your browser. It talks only to your own Salesforce org. We do not run servers that collect your data, we do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we never sell or share your information.

1. What the extension accesses

To do its job, Workbench4Dev accesses the following only while you use it, and only on your device:

  • Your Salesforce session. The extension reads the Salesforce session cookie for the org you have open so it can call Salesforce APIs (REST, Tooling, Metadata and SOAP) as you, with your existing permissions. The session identifier is used in memory for those requests. It is never stored by us, never logged, and never sent anywhere except back to your own Salesforce domain.
  • Salesforce org data you choose to view. This can include object and field metadata, permissions, flows, Apex classes and triggers, debug logs, setup audit trail entries, and — when you run queries or open record-related tools — record data. This data is fetched directly from Salesforce to your browser and displayed there.
  • Browser tab information. The extension checks which open tabs are Salesforce pages so it can detect your connected org and open its tools next to your org tab.

2. What is stored on your device

Workbench4Dev saves a small amount of data locally in your browser (extension storage and browser local/session storage) to make the tool convenient:

  • Preferences (theme, collapsed menu sections, panel layout, opt-ins)
  • Recently viewed items and tool history (for example Developer Console query/Apex history and code-analyzer scan history)
  • Tags, ignore lists and audit-log entries that you create inside the extension
  • Temporary Ask-Your-Org conversation history for the current browser session

This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party. You can remove it at any time by clearing the extension's data in Chrome or by uninstalling the extension.

3. What is sent off your device

Salesforce data and your session are never sent to the developer or to any third-party server. Network requests made by the extension go to:

  1. Your own Salesforce domains (for example *.salesforce.com, *.force.com, *.salesforce-setup.com) — to read the org data you ask to see, and to perform actions you explicitly trigger (such as enabling a debug trace flag or running code in a sandbox).
  2. Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com) — only if you choose to submit the in-app "Help & Feedback" form. See section 4.

There are no analytics, crash reporters, advertising SDKs or remote code in the extension.

4. Feedback form

When you press Send Feedback, the extension sends the following to the Workbench4Dev team through the third-party form service Web3Forms:

  • Your message and the category you selected (feature request, bug report or general question)
  • Your email address, only if you type one (it is optional and used only to reply to you)
  • Optional diagnostics, only if the "Include diagnostics" box is ticked: extension version, Salesforce API version, the connected org's domain name, the active panel name and your browser's user-agent string. Diagnostics never include your Salesforce records, credentials or session information.

We use this information solely to understand and respond to your feedback and to improve Workbench4Dev. Web3Forms processes the submission to deliver it to us; see the Web3Forms privacy policy for how they handle data. Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal data in your message.

5. Why each permission is needed

Permission Why it is needed
cookies Read the Salesforce session cookie of the org you are using, so API requests run with your own login. No cookies are read from other sites.
storage Save your preferences, history and tags locally on your device.
tabs Detect open Salesforce tabs, identify the connected org, and open Workbench4Dev tools beside your org tab.
Salesforce host access (*.salesforce.com, *.force.com, *.salesforce-setup.com, *.lightning.force.com, *.visualforce.com, *.my.site.com, *.cloud.force.com) Call Salesforce APIs for your org and show the small launcher tab on Salesforce pages.
api.web3forms.com Deliver feedback you choose to submit (section 4).

6. Chrome Web Store "Limited Use" commitment

The use of information received from Chrome APIs and Salesforce will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Data is used only to provide and improve the single purpose of the extension: analyzing and exploring your Salesforce org.
  • Data is not sold or transferred to third parties, except as described in section 4 for feedback you submit.
  • Data is not used for advertising, creditworthiness or lending decisions, or unrelated purposes.
  • No human reads your Salesforce data; it never leaves your browser.

7. Security

All communication with Salesforce and Web3Forms uses HTTPS. The extension runs with a strict content security policy (script-src 'self'), loads no remote scripts, and keeps your session token only in memory for the duration of requests. Because the tool acts with your Salesforce permissions, you should use it only on orgs you are authorized to access, and take the same care as you would with any Salesforce admin tool.

8. Data retention and your choices

  • Local data: kept until you clear it or uninstall the extension.
  • Feedback submissions: kept only as long as needed to respond and improve the product. You can ask us to delete a submission you sent by opening an issue in this repository's Issues tab (do not post personal details publicly — say that you'd like a private follow-up).
  • Control: you can disconnect at any time by logging out of Salesforce, closing the org tab, or removing the extension.

9. Children's privacy

Workbench4Dev is a professional developer tool and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
